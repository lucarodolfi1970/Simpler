SIMPLER 1.0.1  —  18 October 2026
=================================

NEW FEATURES
------------
- Caustics in the Rendering panel with a single control: Off / Normal / Sharp (MNEE).
  "Off" turns off all caustics (faster, cleaner render); with "Normal" and "Sharp"
  the details remain available (reflective and refractive caustics).
- Cleaner top bar: the "Caustics" button and the path tracing progress bar were removed
  (progress stays in the badge at the bottom of the view, caustics in the Rendering panel).

BUG FIXES
---------
- Red message "Cannot communicate with the engine: An error occurred while sending the request"
  appearing during the path tracing preview: the engine now accepts more simultaneous requests and
  the app automatically retries requests that were refused for a moment.
- Objects moved in the tree into the "Floor" group were treated as floor
  (excluded from "Drop" and from framing).
