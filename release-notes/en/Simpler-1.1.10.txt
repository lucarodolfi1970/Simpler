SIMPLER 1.1.10  —  18 October 2026
==================================

BUG FIXES
---------
- Very slow final render on models with very large textures (an interior with 442 textures: 1920×1080
  estimated at 50 minutes). When the original textures take more memory than the graphics card has, Cycles
  must work in the computer's memory, many times slower. Now the final render picks by itself (Auto) the
  largest textures that fit in video memory: for example copies at 2048 pixels, which for a 2K image lose
  no detail. The copies are prepared only once and stay in the cache.
- Applying a material made the 3D view be prepared again from scratch: now the material appears at once,
  in every case (a material with textures the view does not have yet appears in its base colour until the
  view is prepared again for other reasons).
- In the final render screen the phase text overlapped the percentage and time.

NEW FEATURES
------------
- Final render › Textures: Auto (recommended) / Full / 4K / 2K.
- "Resources" button at the top: in the sidebar, video memory, graphics card usage and temperature,
  processor, computer and engine memory, and how much video memory the scene textures would need
  (originals, at 2048 and at 1024 pixels), with an indication of whether they fit on the card. Updated every second.
- Accordion sidebar sections: click the heading to close or open them (the state is remembered).
- Diagnostics: Simpler's logs record the reason for each new preparation of the 3D view, the device used by
  the final render (GPU) and its phases.
