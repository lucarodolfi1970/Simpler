SIMPLER 1.1.12  —  20 October 2026
==================================

SPEED
-----
- Path tracing after a change: moving an object or changing a light or a material no longer makes the whole
  scene be prepared again. Cycles, the way Simpler uses it, "baked" the position of each object into its
  geometry and at every change redid everything from scratch (about 2.4 seconds on a large interior with a fast
  card, much more with slower cards). Now each object has an invisible "twin" that shares its
  geometry: this way Cycles keeps it on the card and updates only the position. Measured with Blender 5.2.2 on
  a large interior (on CPU): object moved from 7.6 to 0.55 seconds, material changed from 7.4
  to 0.15 seconds, light changed 0.16 seconds. The twins are not visible, do not appear in the tree, do not go
  into the 3D view and are removed from the final render, which stays at full speed.
- Already prepared 3D view reused: when reopening a project (or after a restart, or undoing a change) the 3D
  view is no longer prepared from scratch if the scene is the same. The last 4 prepared views are kept in
  cache: on a large interior this saves the 20 seconds of preparation.
- Opening projects with embedded textures: the textures are no longer all re-read into memory at every
  opening to recognise them (about 1 second less on a large interior).

BUG FIXES
---------
- Preparing the 3D view after using path tracing: all the original textures (hundreds of 4K/8K) were
  decoded for nothing, taking minutes or running out of memory. Now only the textures really
  used by the materials are read.
- Two projects with images of the same name could swap textures in the cache during the same session.

DIAGNOSTICS
-----------
- The logs record the opening time of the project and the loading time of the 3D view, and when the view comes
  from the cache.
