SIMPLER 1.1.4  —  18 October 2026
=================================

NEW FEATURES
------------
- Start-up screen: on opening, the Simpler splash screen appears with progress at the bottom right
  (render engine start, project opening, 3D view preparation and loading).
- All loading happens behind the splash screen: the app appears only when it is ready, already
  maximized to full screen, with the scene visible in the 3D view.
- If start-up needs an answer (for example where Blender is) the splash screen closes and
  the app shows the question at once.
