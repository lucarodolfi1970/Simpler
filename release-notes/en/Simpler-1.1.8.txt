SIMPLER 1.1.8  —  18 October 2026
=================================

BUG FIXES
---------
- Path tracing still slow on models with textures embedded in the FBX file (for example an
  interior with 442 textures, over 26 GB of video memory). The reduced copies were not made because those
  textures are inside the project and not in a folder. Now embedded textures are also reduced only
  once and kept in the cache: the preview uses copies at 2048 pixels, or 1024 when there are so many
  textures that they would not fit comfortably in the graphics card memory (Auto).
- The reduced copies for the 3D view were redone at every preparation ("Reducing textures for the view
  127 of 420"): now these stay in the cache too, and the second time preparation is much faster.
- "Loading textures" without progress: Cycles reports nothing during that phase. Progress is now
  derived from the memory the graphics card fills (≈ textures loaded, bar, time left).

NEW FEATURES
------------
- Message boxes (confirmations, errors, questions), Settings, Final render and Rename in the app's
  style: dark panel with rounded corners, its own title bar with the small ×.
  Enter = main button, Esc = cancel.
- Thin, dark scroll bars throughout the app, instead of the white Windows ones.
