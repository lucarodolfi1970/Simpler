SIMPLER 1.1.9  —  18 October 2026
=================================

BUG FIXES
---------
- Off-centre orbit with the right button: the view orbited around a centre left at its previous distance
  (after moving with the wheel or the arrows it ended up in empty space, in front of or behind the objects),
  so the framing "ran off" to the side. Now, when the right button is pressed, the orbit centre moves
  to the first surface found exactly in the middle of the view: you orbit around what you are
  looking at. The view does not move when the button is pressed (the centre moves only along the
  line of sight). If there is nothing in the middle, the previous centre is kept.
