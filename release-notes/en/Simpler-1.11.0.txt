SIMPLER 1.11.0  —  21 October 2026
==================================

- ENVIRONMENT: new button in the top bar (and Resources now comes before Camera). It uses Blender's physical
  sky: sun, sky colour, clouds and haze come from the same atmosphere, so they agree with each other and between
  real time and path tracing.
  · Presets: Clear morning, Midday, Afternoon, Golden hour, Sunset, Overcast, Fog, Night, Studio
    (also in the Environments tab of the library below).
  · Sun: time, day of the year, latitude, north, intensity and size (sharper or softer shadows). Colour and
    strength follow the atmosphere: orange at sunset, weaker with haze or clouds.
  · Sky: air, haze (dust and moisture), ozone, altitude and sky light.
  · Clouds: coverage, size, softness and "Other clouds".
  · Haze and fog: distant things fade into the air, all the way to thick fog.
  · Night: stars and moon.
  · Ground: grass, soil, concrete, neutral or none, all the way to the horizon («Ground» object in the tree: it
    can also have another material).
  · Automatic exposure, like a camera: at sunset and at night the image stays readable. It adds to the
    exposure in the rendering settings.
- NEW SCENE: starts outdoors, in full daylight, with grass to the horizon and a light haze, instead of the grey
  floor on a black background. Projects already saved stay as they are until you choose an environment.
- PERFORMANCE: no volumes. The sun is a real light (sharp shadows without noise), the clouds are painted into the
  sky (about 10% more time), the haze is added to the image (no cost in path tracing). The sky of the real-time
  view is prepared by the preview Blender in a couple of seconds, without stopping the rest.
- Saved scenes (Scenes tab) and Undo also include the Environment.
