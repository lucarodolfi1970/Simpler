SIMPLER 1.11.2  —  21 October 2026
==================================

- GLASS AND WATER LET THE LIGHT THROUGH: under water and behind glass everything stayed dark (the bottom of a pond
  black, the shadow of a glass table black). Cycles does not let the direct light of lamps and sun cross a refracting
  surface: it only arrived by chance, as a caustic, and from the sun practically never. Now, as photorealistic
  renderers do, glass and water are transparent (with their colour) for the light that falls on them, while to the
  eye they stay real glass and water: the bottom of the water is lit by the sun, with its shadows. Almost no cost.
  With "Caustics" on, the real caustics are computed instead (the shortcut switches off, the light is not counted
  twice). No need to change the materials: it works for every glass and water in the scene, imported ones too.
- FOG: leaves and other pieces with transparency against the sky became fog-coloured ghosts, and there was a dark
  line at the horizon. The distance of these points is now computed correctly; the haze is also more precise with
  thick fog.
- TOP BAR: in a narrow window the buttons ran out of the bar (Draft/High/Final cut off). Now, when there is no room,
  the labels of the panel buttons go first (the icons stay, with their tooltip on hover), then those of New/Import/
  Open/Save, then the project name: all the buttons stay.
- RELEASE NOTES IN ENGLISH: they are now copied into the folder of the built program too (before, the Italian
  version showed even with Simpler in English).
