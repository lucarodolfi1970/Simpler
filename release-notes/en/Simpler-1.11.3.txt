SIMPLER 1.11.3  —  21 October 2026
==================================

BUG FIXES
---------
- BLACK GLASS AND WATER (also from under the water, and a light under the water could not be seen): in the project
  the Rendering settings had "Total bounces" at 0. Cycles counts every bounce in the total: at 0 the ray stopped on
  the surface of the water or glass, which turned black whatever the material. Now, when the scene has glass or
  water, the path tracing uses at least the "Refractions" value for the total: the water is transparent from above
  (the lit bottom shows) and from below (bottom and sky through the surface, as in reality).
  Cost: only the rays that really cross glass or water go on, the others keep their own limits.
  The vase of the apartment was transparent because that project had normal bounces.
- Rendering panel: a warning when "Total" is below "Refractions" and when "Diffuse" is at 0 (without indirect light
  the areas in shadow, the shaded bottom too, stay black).
