SIMPLER 1.13.10  —  22 October 2026
===================================

DISTRIBUTIONS: A NEW DISTRIBUTION OF THE SAME MODELS IS IMMEDIATE
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- Every new distribution loaded the models from the library again, and took longer each time: in your test with 7
  buildings 9 seconds the first, 30 the second, 60 the third. Also the textures ended up in memory two and three
  times, and the view had to prepare the models again.
- Now, if a model is already in another distribution, the new one uses a copy that shares geometry, materials and
  textures: the distribution is made at once and the view has nothing new to prepare.
