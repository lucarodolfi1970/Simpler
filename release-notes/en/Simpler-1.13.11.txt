SIMPLER 1.13.11  —  22 October 2026
===================================

DISTRIBUTIONS: YOU SEE WHAT IS HAPPENING
----------------------------------------
- While Simpler makes a distribution (the first one with big models takes a few seconds: the models come from the
  library) the small bar appears at the bottom of the view: "Making the distribution…", with the model being
  loaded and how many are left (for example "3 of 7 · Building_C"). Changing one already made: "Updating the
  distribution…".
- In the scene tree the row "New distribution · being prepared…" appears at once, and becomes the real
  distribution as soon as it is ready. Before, for a while nothing was shown and the distribution seemed missing.
- The bar appears only if the operation lasts more than a moment: quick changes stay clean.
