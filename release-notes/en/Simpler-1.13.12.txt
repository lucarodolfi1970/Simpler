DISTRIBUTIONS: THE NAME SAYS HOW THEY WERE MADE
-----------------------------------------------
- New distributions are called "Scatter Brush 1", "Scatter Area 1", "Scatter Spacing 1" (in Italian "Distribuzione
  Pennello 1", "Distribuzione Area 1", "Distribuzione Spaziatura 1"), numbered per type. Before, they were all
  "Scatter 1, 2, 3".
- Distributions already made keep their name; you can always rename them from the tree.
