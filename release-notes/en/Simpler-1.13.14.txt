SIMPLER 1.13.14  —  22 October 2026
===================================

LIBRARY: THE "SIMPLER" MENU IS GONE
-----------------------------------
- The "Simpler ▾" menu is gone: the library menus are Poly Haven ▾ · Personal ▾ · Lights · Scenes.
- Simpler's materials are the "Simpler" folder inside Personal · Materials, next to your folders (with the same
  subfolders as before: Surfaces, Glass, Gems…). Search finds both yours and Simpler's.
- The primitives (cube, sphere, plane…) are the "Primitives" folder inside Personal · 3D models.
- The two folders cannot be moved, renamed or deleted, and do not take dragged materials or models.
