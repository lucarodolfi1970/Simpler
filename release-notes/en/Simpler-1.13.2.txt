SIMPLER 1.13.2  —  22 October 2026
==================================

MORE ORDER: WATER IS A MATERIAL
-------------------------------
- Water is now set in one place only: in the MATERIAL EDITOR, section "Water" (the first one).
  · "Is water": the material becomes clear water (100% transparency, index 1.333, smooth); then you can change it
    like any material. It applies to every object with that material.
  · Below: Real waves (height, size), Colour and clarity (Pool/Lake/Sea/Marsh, clarity, murkiness).
  · The caustics stay a render setting: Rendering → Caustics «Sharp» (the section reminds it).
- The water section is gone from the object properties (it no longer appears on window glass).
- Projects saved with earlier versions update by themselves: the waves move from the object to its material.
- The "Water" material of the library comes with "Is water" already on.
- Undo (Ctrl+Z) also covers the water changes.

BUG FIXES
---------
- WATER FULL OF SPECKLES IN THE PATH TRACING (also with murkiness 0 and without glass): the view stopped after 4
  seconds of its last pass, with few samples (the badge still said 512), and the OptiX denoiser left the grain on
  waves and glass. Now:
  · the last pass reaches all the samples set (a command or a movement interrupts it at once);
  · the denoiser is OpenImageDenoise (on the graphics card), cleaner on water, glass and volumes, also in the final
    render.
  In an indoor pool the water surface is clean already at 32 samples.
- Windows made of many panes joined in one object were taken for "solid glass" (a caustic lens). Now the real
  thickness of the glass counts (volume/surface): thin panes always let the sun in.
