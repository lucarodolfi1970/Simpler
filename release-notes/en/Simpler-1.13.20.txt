SIMPLER 1.13.20  —  22 October 2026
===================================

BUG FIXES
---------
- Right click on the folders, materials and models of the library no longer opened any menu (since 1.13.18): the
  "Paste here" menu of the empty part of the library blocked the menus of the tiles too. Now every tile has its
  menu again (with Cut), and "Paste here" appears only on the empty part.
- MEMORY AFTER "NEW" OR "OPEN": the 3D view kept everything it had loaded for the previous project (the models of the
  distributions with their levels of detail, textures, materials): after a heavy scene, many GB stayed in use even
  with the new empty scene. Now New and Open make the view release everything.
