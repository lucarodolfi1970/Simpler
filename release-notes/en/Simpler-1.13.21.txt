SIMPLER 1.13.21  —  22 October 2026
===================================

NEW MATERIAL TYPE: DISPLACEMENT
-----------------------------------------
- The material "Type" has Displacement: the surface really moves with the height map (stones, bricks, bark,
  terrain). The panel has the "True relief" group at the top and all the channels of Standard below. Standard no
  longer has the Displacement section; materials that had it on are already of type Displacement.
- True relief:
  · Mode: True + detail (movement plus the fine relief, recommended) · True · Bump only (no geometry, fast).
  · Height (map), Maximum height in cm, Zero level (so the object neither floats nor sinks).
  · Vector map (optional): also moves the surface sideways (overhangs, rocks, tiles), with strength and zero.
- Subdivision (Blender 5 adaptive subdivision):
  · In pixels (finer near the camera) or In centimetres (same detail for every copy: distributions).
  · Maximum level, Close the cracks (duplicate vertices merged before moving), Remesh (voxel remesh for models with
    very bad topology; costly, UVs are lost), Less detail off screen.
  · Only in the final render: the path tracing of the view keeps only the bump, fast.

BUG FIXES
---------
- Primitives (cube, sphere, plane, cylinder, cone) had all their UV coordinates at zero: textures did not show and
  displacement did nothing. Now they have real UVs (primitives already in scenes stay as they are).
