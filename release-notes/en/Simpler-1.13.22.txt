SIMPLER 1.13.22  —  22 October 2026
===================================

SAFETY: SIMPLER CAN NO LONGER FREEZE THE COMPUTER
-------------------------------------------------
- What happened: "Remesh" with 2 cm voxels on the Ground (4 × 4 km) asked for about 40 billion faces. Blender filled
  the whole RAM and Windows stopped.
- Before applying them, Simpler estimates how much geometry the heavy Displacement options would make (Remesh,
  subdivision in centimetres, the type itself) on every object using the material. If it is too much (over 15
  million faces for the remesh, 60 million for the subdivision) a message says which object and how many faces, and
  proposes the finest value that fits (for the Ground: 103 cm voxels). "No" changes nothing.
- Even if such a request arrived anyway (old projects, other ways), the engine does not apply a remesh or a
  subdivision beyond the limit. Opening a project saved with too heavy settings, they are taken off.
- Memory limit: Blender and the processes it starts (previews, exports) can use at most three quarters of the RAM,
  always leaving at least 3 GB to Windows. If any operation asks for more, the engine stops, not the computer:
  Simpler says so and starts again reopening the last saved project.
- Esc when the engine has not answered for more than 4 seconds: Simpler asks whether to stop it (a Blender
  computation like the remesh cannot be interrupted halfway: the engine stops, all memory is freed and Simpler
  starts again from the last save).
- The maximum subdivision level of the material now also caps the whole scene.
