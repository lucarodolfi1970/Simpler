SIMPLER 1.13.24  —  22 October 2026
===================================

CLEARER MATERIAL EDITOR
-----------------------
- Right under the Type, the editor says which objects the material is on ("On: Ground, Plane and 3 more"). If it is on
  no object of the scene it says so in orange: "changes do not show in the view". Before, this was at the bottom,
  under the preview, and it happened to edit a material other than the one of the object being looked at (so the
  mapping changes seemed not to work).
- The scale of the mapping is called "Scale" in every mode: in UV it is "Scale · repeats" (how many times it repeats
  on the model's UVs), in Box, Planar, Cylinder and Sphere it is "Scale · real size" in cm.
