SIMPLER 1.13.26  —  22 October 2026
===================================

SHARED MATERIALS: CHANGES GO ONLY ON THE OBJECT
-----------------------------------------------
- Before: Ground and Sphere with the same material; opening the material from the Ground and making it red turned the
  sphere red too, because it was the same material.
- Now, when you open a material from an object and other objects use it too, under the Type there is "Edit: Only
  Ground · All (2)". With "Only …" (default) at the first change the object gets its own copy of the material
  ("Bianco · Ground"): the other objects do not change. With "All" the changes apply to all of them, as before.
- The copy does not need to be saved to the library to exist: it is in the project, tied to that object. To give it
  to other objects drag the preview sphere onto them; to keep it for other projects too, "Save to library".
- The "Only for this object" button is no longer needed and was removed.
