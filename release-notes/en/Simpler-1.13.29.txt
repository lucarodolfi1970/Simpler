SIMPLER 1.13.29  —  22 October 2026
===================================

ENVIRONMENT: CLEARER AND NICER
------------------------------
- Presets (Clear morning, Midday, Sunset, Night, Studio, HDRI…) are tiles with a small coloured sky and their icon; the chosen one is highlighted.
- Every section (Sun, Sky, Clouds, Haze and fog, Night, Ground, Exposure) has its own coloured icon in the title.
- Every parameter has a small icon (time, day, latitude, north, air, ozone, altitude…).
- The Time slider shows night, dawn, day and sunset; the Day of the year slider shows winter and summer.
- Grounds have a dot with their colour.
