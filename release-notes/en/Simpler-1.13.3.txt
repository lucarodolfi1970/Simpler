SIMPLER 1.13.3  —  22 October 2026
==================================

MATERIALS: TYPE "STANDARD" OR "WATER"
-----------------------------------------------
- In the material editor, right under the name, there is "Type": Standard · Water.
  · Standard = the usual material (colour, roughness, transparency, maps…).
  · Water = only the water parameters are shown: Colour and depth (Pool/Lake/Sea/Marsh, clarity, murkiness) and
    Surface and waves (real waves, height, size, index of refraction). The others, not needed for water, are hidden.
  · Back to Standard the material stays as it is and all parameters come back.
- More types will come here later (for example vegetation, fabric).

WATER ALSO IN CLOSED VOLUMES
----------------------------
- Water works on closed objects too (sphere, tank, glass, aquarium), not only on planes.
  Waves form only on the top; the water body sits inside the shape.
- Fixed the halo around a water sphere: the closed object was "extruded" downward like a pool.

NEW SCENE AND GROUND
--------------------
- A new scene starts with a WHITE MATTE ground (new ground type "White" in the Ambience panel).
- Primitives (cube, sphere, plane…) are created with the "White" material: one click opens the material.
