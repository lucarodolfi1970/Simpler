SIMPLER 1.13.31  —  23 October 2026
===================================

COLOURED GLASS THAT CASTS ITS COLOUR
------------------------------------
- A glass with a texture in the Colour channel (a stained glass window, a mosaic) colours the light that passes through it: its colours appear on the floor and around, as with church windows. Before, the light passed white.
- It works for the sun and the lights of the scene, with or without «Thin wall», also for the glass of projects already saved.

GLASS ON A PLANE
----------------
- If the glass is on a sheet without thickness (a plane, a pane) and «Thin wall» is off, the editor says so and offers «Turn on thin wall»: as solid glass a plane looks like a mirror, because the light enters a block of glass that never ends.
