SIMPLER 1.13.32  —  23 October 2026
===================================

3D LIBRARY: OVERWRITING A MODEL NO LONGER LOSES ITS TEXTURES
------------------------------------------------------------
- Saving a model over the existing one (for example after scaling it) deleted its texture folder before copying them: the saved model came back all white. Now the textures stay and the saved model has them.
- The same for «Import» with replace.

THUMBNAILS OF IMPORTED MODELS
-----------------------------
- Lights of other programs (V-Ray, Corona) in an FBX became empty objects far from the model: the thumbnail framed them too and the model looked like a dot. Now they are not imported and the thumbnail frames only what is visible.

DELETING A LIBRARY MODEL IS IMMEDIATE
-------------------------------------
- Deleting a library model from the scene prepared the whole 3D view again (20 seconds in a big scene). Now it disappears at once.
