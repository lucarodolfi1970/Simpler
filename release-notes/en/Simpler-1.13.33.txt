SIMPLER 1.13.33  —  23 October 2026
===================================

IMPORT MODELS INTO THE LIBRARY: THE SCALE
-----------------------------------------
- New «Scale for all» row in step 3: ×1 = as in the file. Type any factor (e.g. 25.4) or pick a quick one: ×0.01, ×0.1, ×2.54, ×10, ×25.4, ×100. It applies to every model of the list.
- New «Check the scale after each model» switch: after each import Simpler stops and shows the thumbnail and the size (width × depth · height in mm, cm, m or km). Type a factor for that model only, see at once how big it will be and press Continue for the next one, or Stop.
  With «Use this factor for the next models too» the correction applies to the rest of the list and Simpler does not stop any more.
- For a file with several models (collections) the factor applies to all the models of that file.
- Every row shows the size of the model after the import.
