SIMPLER 1.13.4  —  22 October 2026
==================================

MATERIAL TYPE: DROP-DOWN LIST
-----------------------------
- "Type" is now a drop-down list with icon and description: Standard (sphere) · Water (blue drop).
- Going back to Standard the material returns as it was before the water: the transparency, index of refraction,
  roughness and metal of before (the other changes stay). Before, it stayed transparent like water.
- The preview sphere updates when you change type (before, it kept the old one).

BUG FIXES
---------
- WATER TURNING WHITE/GREY when raising the waves: 50 cm waves 60 cm wide are spikes, not waves, and the water
  became a grey mirror of the sky; on huge surfaces (the ground is 4 km) the geometry cannot even draw waves that
  small. Now the real height is at most a fifth of the size and drops on surfaces too large; the panel says how much
  it was reduced. For higher waves increase the size too.
- Dragging a library material onto an object, the material editor open on that object switches to the new
  material (before, it stayed on the old one and it looked as if nothing had changed).
