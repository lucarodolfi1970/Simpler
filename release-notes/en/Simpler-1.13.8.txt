SIMPLER 1.13.8  —  22 October 2026
==================================

TOUCHPAD
--------
- Two fingers now move the view right away, without first using the mouse wheel. From the log of your PC: the
  touchpad was recognised correctly, but the movement was measured on the orbit centre, which after opening a scene
  can be a few centimetres from the camera, so the view barely moved. Now the scene follows the fingers at the
  distance of what is under the cursor (or, on the sky, at the size of the scene).
