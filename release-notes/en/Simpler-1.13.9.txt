SIMPLER 1.13.9  —  22 October 2026
==================================

DISTRIBUTIONS OF BIG MODELS: FASTER, NO MORE BUILDINGS DISAPPEARING
-------------------------------------------------------------------
- Why they disappeared: a building of a kit is made of hundreds of pieces, and the view drew every piece separately
  (112 buildings = over 12,000 draws per frame, 16 fps). The performance protection saw the slow view and, taking
  the triangles for too many, hid the farthest pieces; but the problem was not the triangles (3 million are fine on
  a recent graphics card), it was the number of draws.
- Now the pieces of a model with the same material become a single geometry when the model reaches the view: one
  draw per material instead of per piece. Test: a model of 40 pieces with 2 materials goes from 40 to 2 draws per
  level of detail.
- The performance protection no longer hides an object taller than 2% of the view: far buildings stay, at most in
  the lightest version. Far blades of grass still disappear as before.
- Path tracing does not change: everything is always there.
