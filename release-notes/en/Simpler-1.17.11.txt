SIMPLER 1.17.11  —  24 October 2026
===================================

COLOURS FROM A PHOTO: SUNRISE AND OVERCAST
------------------------------------------
- Two new groups of presets in Sky › Advanced › "Colours from a photo": "Sunrise" (4 photos) and "Overcast" (4 photos), next to the 4 sunsets. The list goes Sunrise, Sunset, Overcast, then your photos.
- Sunrise photos put the sun in the morning, Sunset photos in the evening; Overcast photos always choose a covered sky (dark grey alone could look like the shadow of a few big cumuli).
- Similarity of the new presets: from 71 to 90%.
