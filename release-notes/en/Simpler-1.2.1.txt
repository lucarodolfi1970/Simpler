SIMPLER 1.2.1  —  20 October 2026
=================================

NEW FEATURES
------------
- Two clear gestures for materials in the library at the bottom:
  · DRAG the material onto an object in the view = applies it (Shift: to the whole group);
  · CLICK the material = it opens in the right sidebar for editing (it is not applied).
  The same goes for Poly Haven textures. In an object's properties just click the name of its material
  to edit it (no more "Edit" button).
- Material PREVIEW in the editor: a sphere on a checkered backdrop, rendered with path tracing, that
  updates after every edit, even for a material not yet used in the scene. It is computed by a second
  Blender that is always ready, so the scene's path tracing view is not disturbed (with the old method
  every preview would have made the whole scene be prepared again). The same process prepares the library
  thumbnails.
- SAVE to the library: by default it overwrites the library material it comes from; "Save as new…"
  asks for a name and creates a new material in the library (the original goes back to how it was saved). The
  editor shows whether there are unsaved changes. The library is in %APPDATA%\Simpler\materials, with a copy
  of the textures, and applies to all projects. Right-click a library material: "Restore Simpler's
  material" (for modified ones) or "Remove from the library" (for ones you created).
- More natural, adjustable DISPERSION: "Intensity" (0-100%, default 35%) is the share of light that splits into
  colours; "Colour separation" (1× = real value) is how far apart they spread. Before, the effect was very strong
  even at the minimum: Cycles picks the colour again at every surface it passes through, and with all the light
  split into three colours a gem became dark and full of coloured specks. Now part of the light stays normal
  glass: this is why sharp caustics (MNEE) also work with dispersion (white).
- Number boxes: Enter confirms the typed value at once (even beyond the slider limits, upwards, and
  well below the minimum for logarithmic sliders).
