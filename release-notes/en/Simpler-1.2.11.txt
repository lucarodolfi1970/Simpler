SIMPLER 1.2.11  —  20 October 2026
==================================

NEW FEATURES
------------
- TWO GIZMOS instead of three, smaller:
  · "Move and rotate" (W, also E): arrows to move and three arcs to rotate, together; where
    arrows and arcs touch, the one closest to the mouse wins;
  · "Scale" (R): handles with small cubes.
  The top toolbar now has two buttons.
- RESOURCES > Graphics card: new "Who uses the video memory" list, program by program (Simpler's engine, preview
  process, 3D view, other programs), with the same numbers as Task Manager. The "Video memory" value at the top is
  for the whole card, that is for all open programs, not just Simpler.
- Poly Haven folders (materials and 3D models): if they contain something already downloaded, a small green count
  out of the total appears (e.g. 3/88).

FIXES
-----
- POLY HAVEN MODELS WITHOUT TEXTURES (e.g. Cannon 01): some Poly Haven .blend files (the models with a skeleton for
  animation) have materials without any image. Now, when the .blend has no textures, Simpler downloads the glTF
  version, which always has them. Models already downloaded that way are downloaded again automatically the next
  time they are used (delete the untextured cannon from the scene and insert it again).
- From Poly Haven .blend files, the shapes that draw the skeleton's "bones" (wdg_* objects) no longer enter the
  scene, nor do skeletons, cameras and lights from glTF/FBX files.
