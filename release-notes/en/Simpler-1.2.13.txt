SIMPLER 1.2.13  —  20 October 2026
==================================

- GIZMO redone: three axes joined at the centre (positive direction only, arrows at the tips), a yellow
  centre to move freely and three rotation bands that join the tips of the axes (red between blue and green =
  rotate around X, green between blue and red = around Y, blue between red and green = around Z). No more detached
  parts or plane handles. The scale gizmo: axes with a small cube at the tip and a grey centre to scale everything.
  Thicker, more readable axis lines.
- PATH TRACING: the orange selection outline is no longer shown on the path-traced image.
- RIGHT CLICK: it no longer changes the selection if the clicked piece is part of what is selected (for example a
  piece inside the selected "ring_02" group). The menu acts on the whole selection, so "Save to library" saves the
  whole model.
