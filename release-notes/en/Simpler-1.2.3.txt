SIMPLER 1.2.3  —  20 October 2026
=================================

NEW FEATURES
------------
- BOTTOM LIBRARY IN WHOLE ROWS: when you drag its top edge, the library snaps between 1, 2 and 3 rows of tiles
  (never rows cut in half, 3 at most). Double click the edge: next row count (1 → 2 → 3 → 1).
  On small screens some of the 3D view always remains visible.
- RECTANGLE AND LASSO SELECTION in the toolbar at the top left of the view:
  · "▭ Rectangle": hold down the left button and drag a rectangle;
  · "◌ Lasso": hold down the left button and draw a freehand shape.
  Every piece (lights included) whose centre, as you see it on screen, falls inside the shape is selected.
  Ctrl or Shift add to the selection, Alt removes. A simple click still selects a single piece.
  While one of the two is active the left button draws (right and middle buttons still move the view; the gizmo is
  paused); click the button again or press Esc to go back to normal navigation.
- THE MATERIAL EDITOR FOLLOWS THE CLICK: with the material editor (or the list of scene materials) open on the
  right, clicking an object in the view opens the material of the clicked surface (the tree on the left jumps to
  the object as before). With the object's properties open, everything works as before.
- DRAG THE PREVIEW SPHERE onto an object in the view: it applies the editor's material, exactly as from the bottom
  library (to the piece under the mouse; Shift: the whole group).
- While dragging a material (library, Poly Haven, preview sphere) a small ball with the material's preview and its
  name appears next to the pointer.
