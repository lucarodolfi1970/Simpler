SIMPLER 1.2.6  —  20 October 2026
=================================

- SCENE TREE, dragging with three zones on each row:
  · top or bottom edge of the row (a blue line appears) = the object goes NEXT TO that row, at the same level: this
    way a child moves back up a level (to bring it to the root, drop it between two root objects, or in the empty
    space below the tree);
  · middle of the row (orange outline) = the object goes INSIDE that row, which becomes its parent.
- OBJECT LIBRARY: an object saved on its own is inserted directly at the root of the tree, with the name given in
  the library (no extra group). Several objects saved together stay in a group with that name.
- (since 1.2.5) Saving a child saves only the child, not its parent: the "Floor" you saw in the library came from
  1.2.4. For a model saved with 1.2.4: right click > "Render the thumbnail again" cleans it up.
