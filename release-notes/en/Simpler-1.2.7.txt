SIMPLER 1.2.7  —  20 October 2026
=================================

- POLY HAVEN 3D MODELS: new tab with the photorealistic models from polyhaven.com (CC0, free for commercial use
  too, more than 500), in folders taken from Poly Haven's categories (e.g. Furniture / Seating), with thumbnails and
  search across all folders. Click = in the middle of the view; drag it into the view = its base goes onto the
  surface where you drop it. It is downloaded on first use (Poly Haven's original .blend file, with materials made
  for Cycles and 2K textures) and then stays on disk (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Simpler\library\polyhaven\models) for all
  projects. The insertion can be undone with Ctrl+Z. If you like it, right click > "Save to library" and you'll
  find it in your 3D models, in the folder you want.
- LIBRARY TABS renamed and reordered: Lights · 3D models · Poly Haven 3D models · Materials ·
  Poly Haven materials (formerly "Poly Haven textures") · Environments · Scenes.
