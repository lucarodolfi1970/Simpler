SIMPLER 1.2.9  —  20 October 2026
=================================

FIXES
-----
- TEXTURES ROTATED BY 90° BETWEEN REAL TIME AND PATH TRACING ("box" projection, for example Poly Haven materials):
  the real-time view read the texture with different axes from Cycles. I measured with test renders how Cycles
  projects onto each face (top/bottom, front/back, right/left, with the right orientation) and the real-time view
  now does the same, including the editor's rotation, scale and offset. Checked by comparing the two views on the
  same framing.
- LIGHT ICONS in the view: they always stay small and the same size on screen. Before, they inherited the scale of
  the light or of the group they are in, and in some scenes they became huge.

NEW FEATURES
------------
- Green tick at the top right of the preview of Poly Haven materials and 3D models already downloaded to the PC
  (they can be used right away, even without Internet). It appears as soon as a download finishes.
