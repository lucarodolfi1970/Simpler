SIMPLER 1.23.0  —  24 October 2026
==================================

- Generated roughness: on the Roughness map, the magic wand offers
  · "Roughness from the colour map": from the brightness of the texture, levelled; dark = rough (90%), bright = smooth (25%). Instant.
  · "Roughness with worn edges": on top, the curvature of the relief (DeepBump, from the normal map or, if missing, from the colour texture) makes edges smoother and hollows rougher, as with real wear.
  If the material works the other way round (bright = rough), use "Invert the map"; "Map weight" blends it with the slider value.
- Glossiness doesn't need generating: it is the inverted roughness.
