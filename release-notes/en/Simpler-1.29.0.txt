SIMPLER 1.29.0  —  25 October 2026
==================================

- New «Licenses and credits» command in the File menu (and in Settings, next to «Info»): a page with every program, library, service and content by other authors that Simpler uses, with their licenses and links — Blender and Cycles, three.js, three-mesh-bvh, meshoptimizer, WebView2, DeepBump, Poly Haven, ambientCG, OpenAI, Sketchfab — the names and trademarks mentioned, the cars recommended from Sketchfab with author and credit to use, and the notes to settle before distributing Simpler.
