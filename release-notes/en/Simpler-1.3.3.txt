SIMPLER 1.3.3  —  20 October 2026
=================================

FIXES
-----
- MATERIAL PREVIEW (the sphere): textures applied with UV coordinates are now really visible. The preview sphere
  had no UV coordinates, so it read a single point of the texture (in the case of the leaf, the black background,
  hence the dark "metallic" sphere).
- LEAVES IN PATH TRACING: no more grey veils around the leaves. Many leaf textures have a background that is only
  partly transparent: real time cuts it at 50%, while path tracing showed it as a dark veil.
  Now path tracing also does the "Clean cut-out" (new option in Opacity, on by default; turn it off for gradual
  transparency: dirty glass, curtains). Projects already saved are fixed when opened.
- TEXTURES WITH TRANSPARENCY: when you choose as colour a texture with transparent parts (leaves, grids, decals),
  "Use the alpha of the colour texture" turns on by itself, so real time and path tracing match.
- "MOVE TO" in the right-click menus (folders and library objects/materials): the submenu didn't open (the dark
  menu theme didn't support submenus). Now it opens to the right, with the arrow and the scroll bar.
- DRAGGING IN THE LIBRARY: when you bring a folder, a model or a material near the left or right edge of the
  library, it scrolls by itself (faster the closer you are to the edge): you can reach folders out of view without
  releasing the button.
