SIMPLER 1.3.6  —  20 October 2026
=================================

FIXES
-----
- PREVIEW VIDEO MEMORY: the second Blender (material previews and model thumbnails) could take up many GB of video
  memory (7.6 GB in the Resources tab). Causes and remedies:
  - it kept on the card everything it had drawn (textures and meshes of every imported or saved model): now the
    card is freed after each image;
  - it read textures at full resolution (even 4K/8K) for 256 px images: now at most 1024 px;
  - it stayed on even when it had nothing to do: now it closes after 45 seconds without work and its video memory
    goes back to zero. The next request restarts it automatically (a few extra seconds, only for that one).
  Previews and thumbnails are, and remain, PNG images saved on disk.
