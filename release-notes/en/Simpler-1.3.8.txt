SIMPLER 1.3.8  —  21 October 2026
=================================

FIXES
-----
- INDEPENDENT FOLDERS: moving the Poly Haven materials also moved the Poly Haven 3D models (which by default are in
  a "models" subfolder of the same folder). Now each library moves on its own:
  - the copy skips the folders of the other libraries;
  - when the old folder is deleted, the folders of the other libraries inside it are left where they are, with all
    their content;
  - the count of files and GB covers only the library you are moving.
- If the Poly Haven 3D models ended up inside the materials folder (as with 1.3.7), use "Change…" on
  "Poly Haven 3D models" (or "Back to default"): they are copied to the folder you choose and at the end
  Simpler can delete the "models" subfolder left in the materials folder.
