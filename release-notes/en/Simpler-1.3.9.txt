SIMPLER 1.3.9  —  21 October 2026
=================================

NEW
---
- GIZMO: MOVING ON A PLANE. The wedge enclosed by two axes and the arc joining them is now a
  handle: hovering over it colours it (with the colour of the arc that closes it) and lights up the two axes;
  dragging moves the object only in those two directions:
    · wedge between X and Y (blue arc): on the floor, without changing the height;
    · wedge between X and Z (green arc) and between Y and Z (red arc): on the two vertical walls.
  The arcs are still for rotating: if the pointer is right on the arc it rotates, inside the wedge it moves.
  The arrow handles are a little thinner, so they leave room for the wedges. Snap and World/Local axes also
  apply to the planes.
