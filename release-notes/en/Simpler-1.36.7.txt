SIMPLER 1.36.7  —  25 October 2026
==================================

- Decal panel reorganised. At the top there are only Size, Depth, Opacity and Colour, plus a line saying which maps the decal has.
- Everything else is in Advanced, in one box per channel, each with its map and only its own values:
  - Relief (normal): map, Strength, DirectX normal;
  - Roughness: map, or the slider when there is no map;
  - Metal: map, or the slider;
  - Transparency: opacity map;
  - Emission (light): Brightness and map;
  - Shape: Width, Height, Order.
