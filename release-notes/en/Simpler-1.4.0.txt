SIMPLER 1.4.0  —  21 October 2026
=================================

NEW
---
- COPY AND PASTE OBJECTS: Ctrl+C copies the selected objects (also from the right-click menu), Ctrl+V opens the
  "Paste" window where you choose:
    · INSTANCE: the copies share shape and materials with the original (in Blender: "linked duplicate").
      Cycles draws them as instances of a single geometry: almost no extra memory even with thousands of
      copies (trees, chairs, street lamps). Changing the material of one changes them all.
    · INDEPENDENT COPY: each copy has its own geometry and you can change its materials one by one; it takes up
      as much memory as the original.
    · QUANTITY: the pasted copies are placed side by side along the X axis.
  Ctrl+D is still the quick duplicate (an instance, no questions asked).
- COPY BY DRAGGING WITH SHIFT: hold Shift and drag the gizmo. The original stays in place (you
  see its "ghost") and the outline follows the mouse; when you release, Simpler asks for the type (instance or
  independent copy) and quantity. The first copy goes where you released, the next ones continue at the same
  distance and in the same direction; dragging a rotation arc arranges the copies in a circle around the centre
  of the gizmo. Can be undone with Ctrl+Z.
  WARNING: inverting Snap while dragging is now done with Ctrl (it used to be Shift).
- GRAPHICS CARD POWER: at startup, on the start screen, the graphics card, its memory and a % rating of how well
  suited it is to Simpler appear (100% = a card for very large scenes, like an RTX 4090 with 24 GB), with a
  verdict (excellent, very good, good, adequate, limited). No test: the data comes from the driver in a few
  milliseconds, in parallel, so startup doesn't slow down. The calculation weighs computing power (cores × clock,
  ray tracing) at 60% and video memory at 40%. The same rating is in the Resources tab.
