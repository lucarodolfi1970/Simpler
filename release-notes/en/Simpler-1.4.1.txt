SIMPLER 1.4.1  —  21 October 2026
=================================

NEW
---
- FPS IN THE VIEW: at the bottom right, in small print, the frames per second of the real-time view, the triangles
  drawn and the draw calls ("draw"). The number turns yellow below 30 fps and red below 15. Hovering over it also
  shows the time needed to prepare a frame. (The maximum is the screen's refresh rate: 60, 144…)
  Hidden while viewing path tracing.
- "BOXES" VIEW (in the top bar, before "Real time"): for huge scenes. Every piece is drawn as a box of its colour,
  oriented like the piece; repeated objects (instances) also get their own box.
  All the boxes together are a single draw call, with no textures or shadows: navigation stays smooth
  even with thousands of objects. Selection, gizmo, copies and menus work as always (clicking uses the real shape);
  selected pieces are orange. Blender has the equivalent ("Display As: Bounds"), but our real-time view isn't
  Blender's, so the mode is built directly into the view.
