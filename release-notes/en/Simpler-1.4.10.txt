SIMPLER 1.4.10  —  21 October 2026
==================================

NEW
---
- ROW ORDER IN THE TREE: dragging a row onto the top or bottom edge of another one (blue line) puts the object
  exactly there, before or after that row, even at the same level (previously the tree was always in alphabetical
  order and moving between two rows of the same level did nothing). The order is saved in the project and can be
  undone with Ctrl+Z. An original takes its instances with it; dropping something on the "N instances" row puts
  it right after the original. The middle of the row (orange border) still means "put inside the group".
  Objects you have never moved stay in alphabetical order, after the ones ordered by hand.
