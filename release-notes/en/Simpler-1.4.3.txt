SIMPLER 1.4.3  —  21 October 2026
=================================

NEW
---
- FPS: BESIDES THE FRAMES SHOWN, HOW MANY THE CARD COULD DRAW. The real-time view draws one frame per screen
  refresh (vsync, like every web page and as WebView2 requires): on a 60 Hz screen the counter stops at 60, even
  with an empty scene. This is intentional: there is no point drawing frames the screen doesn't show, the card
  stays free for path tracing and doesn't heat up.
  The counter now also shows "max ≈": every 20 frames Simpler measures how long the card takes to draw one until
  the work is finished, and works out how many frames per second it could draw with that scene. The colour
  (yellow/red) now follows this value, not the screen's limit.
