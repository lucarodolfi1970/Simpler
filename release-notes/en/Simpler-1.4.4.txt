SIMPLER 1.4.4  —  21 October 2026
=================================

NEW
---
- INSTANCES IN THE TREE: an orange dot on the icon marks the objects that are instances, i.e. that share shape and
  materials with other models (copies made as "Instance", Ctrl+D, Shift+drag). The original has the dot too:
  original and instances are the same thing. On a group, the dot means its pieces are instances of those of another
  group. Hovering over it tells you how many other models it is shared with.
  Parts repeated within a single model (e.g. the four prongs of a ring) don't count: a unique model stays
  without a dot.

FIXES
-----
- LIGHTER INDEPENDENT COPY: parts that share the same shape in the original (e.g. the prongs) keep sharing a single,
  new one in the copy, instead of all becoming separate.
