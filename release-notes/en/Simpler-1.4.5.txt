SIMPLER 1.4.5  —  21 October 2026
=================================

NEW
---
- UNSAVED CHANGES: when you close Simpler, if the scene has unsaved changes you are asked
  "Save them before closing Simpler?" with Yes (saves and closes; for a new project it asks where), No (closes
  without saving) and Cancel (stays open). The same question appears before "New" and, now also, before "Open".
  If nothing has changed since the last save Simpler asks nothing: moving the view, switching between real time
  and path tracing or resizing the window don't count as changes; objects, materials, lights, environment, saved
  views, rendering and camera settings do.
