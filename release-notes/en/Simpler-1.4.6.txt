SIMPLER 1.4.6  —  21 October 2026
=================================

NEW
---
- TIDY TREE WITH INSTANCES:
  · the ORIGINAL no longer has the dot: next to its name it has an orange "×4" label with the number of its
    instances;
  · the INSTANCES are no longer listed one by one at the same level: under the original there is a collapsed
    "4 instances" row (with the orange dot); a click opens it and shows the copies, each with the dot. This way
    even thousands of copies take up a single row;
  · right click on "N instances": "Select the N instances" or "Select the original and all instances";
  · clicking an instance in the view opens its row in the tree automatically;
  · when copying different models, each one has its own group of instances: you can tell right away what is a copy
    of what.
- A copy of a copy is an instance of the same original. Independent copies are models in their own right (no dot).
  Scenes saved earlier: models that share geometry are recognised and the first one by name acts as the
  original.
