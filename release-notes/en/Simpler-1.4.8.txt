SIMPLER 1.4.8  —  21 October 2026
=================================

FIXES
-----
- TREE: ORIGINAL AND INSTANCES MOVE TOGETHER. Dragging the original (or the "N instances" row under it) into
  another group, above or below another row, its instances follow it: they stay grouped under it in the new
  place too, each in its own position in the scene. A single Ctrl+Z undoes the whole move.
  Dropping something on the "N instances" row puts it next to the original (same level).
  Single instances can still be dragged on their own (by opening the "N instances" row): moved elsewhere,
  an instance appears with its orange dot in the new place.
