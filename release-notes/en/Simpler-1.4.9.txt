SIMPLER 1.4.9  —  21 October 2026
=================================

FIXES
-----
- GIZMO AT THE BASE: models imported with "Model origin: Base" really do have their origin at the centre of the
  base (checked also with rotated and scaled objects, like those exported from 3ds Max), but the gizmo still went to
  "3D centre", which was the starting position. Now the gizmo's starting position is "Base (feet)" and
  Simpler remembers the one you choose in the view bar (Base, 3D centre, Origin) in later sessions too.
  With "Origin" the gizmo goes exactly to the origin chosen at import.
- ALWAYS-ON-TOP WINDOWS (texture viewer, library import, dialog boxes): when closed, focus went back to another
  program instead of Simpler. Now it always returns to the main window.
