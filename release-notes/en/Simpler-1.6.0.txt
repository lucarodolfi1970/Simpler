SIMPLER 1.6.0  —  21 October 2026
=================================

SCATTER: FAST EVEN WITH THOUSANDS OF PIECES
-------------------------------------------
- INSTANT BRUSH STROKES: pieces appear right under the brush, with the real model (no more green cones), and stay
  there. Previously every batch made the whole 3D view prepare again (10-17 seconds).
  The engine now creates 200 pieces in about 0.1 seconds.
- The first time you use a model, Simpler prepares a copy of it for the 3D view: a few seconds, then never again,
  even in other projects.
- GPU INSTANCING: in the 3D view all the pieces of a model are drawn from a single copy. 300 bushes = 5 draw
  calls. Pieces outside the frame are not drawn.
- AUTOMATIC LEVELS OF DETAIL (LOD): for each model Simpler prepares three lighter versions by itself
  (about 30%, 8% and 2% of the triangles), without blocking the view.
  · Vegetation (leaves, grass blades, needles): removes whole leaves and enlarges the remaining ones, so the
    canopy keeps its volume and colour from a distance.
  · Trunks, rocks and other continuous surfaces: simplified with meshoptimizer.
  Each piece uses the level suited to its size on screen. If the scene exceeds what the graphics card can draw
  smoothly, Simpler lowers the detail of the farthest pieces, following the measured time of each frame. The
  counter at the bottom right shows how many pieces are visible, and "automatic LOD" when it steps in.
- Path tracing and the final render always use the full models (Cycles draws instances natively).
- Hiding and showing a scatter again from the tree no longer piles the pieces up in one spot.
- The 3D view no longer prepares again for scatters, and the view files are smaller.

SCATTER PANEL
-------------
- Each scatter has its own models: selecting "Scatter 1" or "Scatter 2" in the tree with the brush active, the
  panel shows the models of that scatter. It also works with a reopened project: the models are worked out from
  the pieces, at 100%. Pressing B with a scatter selected also resumes from that one.
- "+ New" empties the models to scatter: the next brush stroke starts a new group.
- At startup, with New and with Open, the brush starts empty (only the adjustments remain: diameter, spacing,
  scale, rotation).
- The button that removes a model from the brush is now a bin, with an explanation on mouse hover.
- "Paint into: …" visible again.

GRAPHICS CARD AT REST
---------------------
- The 3D view only redraws when something changes: you move the view, the mouse, press a key, make an edit, a
  texture arrives. When still, it leaves the graphics card free: no 90% load, less heat, more power for path
  tracing and other programs. The counter shows this with "still".
- The counter no longer shows "2 triangles · 1 draw" when an object is selected: it counts the whole frame.
