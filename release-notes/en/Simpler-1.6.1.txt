SIMPLER 1.6.1  —  21 October 2026
=================================

SCATTER: THE ADJUSTMENTS CHANGE WHAT YOU HAVE ALREADY PAINTED
-------------------------------------------------------------
- A scatter now remembers its brush strokes: where the brush went, with what diameter, and where you erased.
  The pieces are worked out from there with the current adjustments.
  If you move a slider (spacing, random spacing, scale, rotation and their random parts, align to surface)
  or change the presence, add or remove a model, the whole scatter is redone immediately. Example: trees too
  close together, raise Spacing and they thin out.
- The same brush strokes always give the same result. If you change only the presence, the pieces stay where
  they are and some of them change model. If you change scale or rotation, the pieces stay in place.
- Each change is an undo step ("Scatter"): Ctrl+Z goes back to the previous pieces and adjustments.
- Brush strokes, adjustments and models are saved with the project. When you reopen it and select the scatter
  in the tree with the brush active, the panel shows its adjustments and its models with their presences.
- The diameter applies to new brush strokes: the ones already made keep theirs.
- Scatters painted with 1.5 and 1.6.0 have no saved brush strokes: their pieces stay as they are and the
  adjustments apply only to new brush strokes.
- Note: when the scatter is redone, pieces moved or deleted by hand with the gizmo go back to how the brush
  strokes make them.

FIXES
-----
- While you paint, pieces no longer vanish halfway through a brush stroke: they show up right away with their
  model. The first time you use a model they appear as green cones until the model is ready (a few seconds),
  then they turn into the real model.
