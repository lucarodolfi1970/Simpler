SIMPLER 1.62.0  —  28 October 2026
==================================

- New «Lighten» box at the bottom of the scene tree, always in sight, for heavy scenes. Seven buttons, orange when on:
  · Snow: hides the mantle and the flakes, no recomputing while you move models (only when it snows).
  · Distributions: trees, grass, scatters and ecosystems leave the real-time view (they stay in path tracing).
  · Flakes: the falling flakes leave the real-time view (the mantle stays).
  · Shadows: turns off the view's shadows; a second click brings them back as before.
  · Half resolution: the view draws a quarter of the pixels, much smoother (path tracing does not change).
  · Pause computations: rain shelter, mantle and flakes are not made again after each move until you resume them.
  · Free memory.
  Distributions, flakes, shadows and half resolution stay as you left them next time too; snow and computations are always back on when a project opens.
- The «Snow hidden» message in the bottom bar is no longer cut.
