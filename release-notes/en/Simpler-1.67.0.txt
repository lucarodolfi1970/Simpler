SIMPLER 1.67.0  —  28 October 2026
==================================

- Presets for the rain and the snow, in the Advanced boxes. Rain: Light, Moderate, Heavy, Downpour; falling snow: Light, Heavy, Blizzard. One click sets all the values together: drops, wind, splashes, rings in the puddles and drops on the surfaces (for the snow: flakes, wind, gusts, patches, motion blur). «Wet», «Puddles» and the snow on the ground stay as they are.
- Your own presets: «Save…» saves the current settings with the name you want, with its icon. Right click on one of yours: update with the current settings, rename, delete. They are kept for every project.
- The single sliders are in «Adjust by hand», closed at first: the chosen preset stays highlighted until you change a value.
