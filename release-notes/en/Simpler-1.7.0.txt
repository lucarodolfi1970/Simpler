SIMPLER 1.7.0  —  21 October 2026
=================================

LIBRARY MODELS: INSERTED WITHOUT PREPARING THE VIEW AGAIN
---------------------------------------------------------
- Inserting a model from the library (3D models and Poly Haven 3D models), with a click or by dragging it, no
  longer makes the whole 3D view prepare again (previously 7-17 seconds, more on large scenes).
  · The first time you use a model, Simpler prepares a copy of it for the view: a few seconds, then it stays
    ready in other projects too.
  · From the second time on, the model appears immediately.
- The scatter system now applies to all library models and their copies (Ctrl+C/V, Shift-drag, Duplicate):
  · GPU instancing: all copies are drawn from a single copy of the model;
  · automatic levels of detail: copies that are far away or small on screen use lighter versions;
  · copies outside the frame are not drawn;
  · copies appear immediately too, without preparing the view again.
- You can edit inserted models as before:
  · changing a material shows up immediately on all the copies that use it;
  · "Only for this object", hiding or moving a part from the properties: that object gets its own copy for the
    view (a few seconds).
- Path tracing and the final render always use the full models.
- Limitations:
  · in the 3D view a part inside an inserted model cannot be selected on its own: clicking it or choosing it
    in the tree selects the whole model, and the gizmo moves it as a whole;
  · models with bones (rigged characters) and objects imported directly into the scene work as
    before.

SCENES WITH THOUSANDS OF OBJECTS
--------------------------------
- The scene list (tree, sync with the view) with 6,000 objects goes from 26 s to 0.5 s:
  for each object the engine was scanning all the objects in the file again.
- Redoing a scatter of 3,000 pieces (brush sliders): from 6-29 s to 1-2 s.
- 1,000 copies with Shift-drag or Paste: about 1 s.
- Undo/Redo of thousands of pieces: from 3.7 s to about 1 s.
(Times measured without a graphics card: with one they are lower.)
