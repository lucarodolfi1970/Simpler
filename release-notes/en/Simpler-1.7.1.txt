SIMPLER 1.7.1  —  21 October 2026
=================================

NEW
---
- MOVE ON SURFACES: dragging the centre of the gizmo (the yellow square) slides the selection
  over the surfaces under the mouse, always resting on its base: floor, table, terrain, even library models
  and scatters. It is "Drop" while you move it.
  · An object below the floor comes back on top as soon as you grab it by the centre.
  · The label next to the mouse shows "on surface".
  · Holding Alt: free movement as before (even in mid-air). The axis arrows and the plane wedges work as
    usual.
  · With Shift (copies by dragging) movement stays free.
