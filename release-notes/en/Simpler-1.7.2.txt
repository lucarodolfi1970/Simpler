SIMPLER 1.7.2  —  21 October 2026
=================================

FIXES
-----
- MATERIALS ON INSTANCES: dragging a material onto a model that has instances (copies made as "Instance",
  library models and their copies, pieces of a scatter) changes it on all the instances, in path tracing and
  in the real-time view. Previously it changed only the chosen object.
  · To give an object a different material from its instances, use "Only for this object" as before.
  · Ctrl+Z restores the previous material on all of them.
- IN THE REAL-TIME VIEW the new material also shows up immediately on the selected object: previously it
  appeared only after deselecting it. While Simpler prepares the model's new look (a couple of seconds), the
  previous one stays visible, with no gaps.
- SCATTER BRUSH: light green circle, more solid and with a thicker edge, clearly visible even on a light
  floor (red while erasing).
