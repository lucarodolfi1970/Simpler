SIMPLER 1.75.19  —  31 October 2026
===================================

Save the picture of the view.

- In the bar inside the view, after «Lasso», the «⤓ Save image» button saves the picture of the view as it is at that moment to a JPEG file.
  - In path tracing it saves the image reached so far (even halfway through the passes), at the resolution of the view.
  - In real time it saves the view at its full resolution, without gizmo, selection outlines or light icons.
