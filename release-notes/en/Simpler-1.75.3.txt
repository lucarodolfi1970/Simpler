SIMPLER 1.75.3  —  31 October 2026
==================================

Real-time view: no more fixed 30 fps.

- The Qashqai ran at 30 fps even when still, with a video card able to draw 690 frames per second.
  - Cause (from the 1.75.2 log): the view drew every other frame. Something marked the materials as «to be made again» at every frame; the view then skipped the next frame to prepare the shaders in the background, and never found anything new to prepare.
  - Now, after 3 checks in a row with nothing new, the view stops skipping frames, until a frame really prepares a new shader. The protection against long freezes after an import stays.
  - A still view is no longer drawn again and again: the video card stays free.
  - The log says who marks the materials, to remove the cause at its root.
