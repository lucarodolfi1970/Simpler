SIMPLER 1.75.37  —  1 November 2026
===================================

AI video: «Generate automatic audio» option.

- In the AI video panel, under the length, there is the new «Generate automatic audio» box.
- On: the video keeps the audio Krea generates automatically.
- Off, the default: Simpler removes the audio as soon as the video is downloaded, as before; the video does not change.
- The choice made when you press «Create video» is the one used.
