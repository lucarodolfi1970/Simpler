SIMPLER 1.75.47  —  1 November 2026
===================================

A small × in every text box.

- Simpler's text boxes now have a small × at the end that empties the box and puts the cursor back in it: library and tree searches, names, colour look search, texts of the AI functions and the others. It appears only when there is text.
- Number boxes and API key boxes do not have it.
- The AI video «Motion» box keeps its own ×, which can be undone.
