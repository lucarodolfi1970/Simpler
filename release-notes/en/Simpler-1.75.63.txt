SIMPLER 1.75.63  —  1 November 2026
===================================

«Box» and «Lasso» selection: now finds library models too.

- The box and the lasso selected nothing on cars and other models inserted from the library. In the view these models have no geometry of their own: they are drawn from their prepared version, once for all their copies, and the area selection only looked for geometry. Now it counts them too, at their real size: you select the whole model, not its inner pieces.
- Pieces generated by a distribution stay excluded: there are thousands of them, and they are selected with their distribution.
