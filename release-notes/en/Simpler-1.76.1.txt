SIMPLER 1.76.1  —  1 November 2026
==================================

Terrain: zones are no longer semi-transparent.

- Some Poly Haven ground textures come with an opacity map (for example the grass with leaves used for the Lawn): the terrain material took it, and the terrain became partly see-through. Terrain materials are now always solid.
- Terrains already made with 1.76.0 are fixed on their own when the project is opened.
