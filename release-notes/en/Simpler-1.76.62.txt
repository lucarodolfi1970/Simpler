SIMPLER 1.76.62  —  6 November 2026
===================================

Release notes rewritten: they talk only about Simpler.

- All the notes, in Italian and in English, now describe the changes of each version without naming particular projects, scenes or computers: instead of the names of the test projects there are generic descriptions ("a large interior", "a sea scene", "a scene with thousands of objects").
- The internal verification parts ("Tested", "Not tested", the checks before delivery and their results) and the references to the logs of a computer are gone.
- The names of other rendering programs are gone.
- The numbers stay where they explain an improvement (times before and after, memory).
