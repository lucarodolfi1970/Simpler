SIMPLER 1.77.1  —  6 November 2026
==================================

New material type «Fabric»: full cloth with fuzz, or thin cloth that lets you see and lets light through.

- In the material editor, in the type menu, there is «Fabric».
  - Full: velvet, wool, felt, cotton, denim. Fuzz: the soft light veil on the edges of cloth.
  - Thin: curtains, tulle, gauze. Transparency (how much you see through), Denser edge on (seen at a slant the cloth becomes more opaque, like a real curtain), Light through (a curtain with the sun behind lights up).
  - Advanced: Fuzz softness and colour; «Where the fuzz is», a mask to have it only on some parts; «Tint mask» with its colour, to change the colour of stripes or patterns without making the texture again.
  - Switching to Fabric, if the fuzz was at zero it starts at 50%.
  - The channels a cloth needs remain: colour, roughness, dirt, relief, opacity and projection.
  - The same in the real-time view and in path tracing.
