SIMPLER 1.78.4  —  6 November 2026
==================================

The Scatter brush stays smooth even with big radii; the Simpler folders can become read only.

- Scatter brush: with a big radius and a small distance a single touch could ask thousands of objects, and in a few seconds the view froze. Now:
  - at most 2,000 objects per stroke; when more would be needed, the bar below shows the distance that fits that radius;
  - a distribution has a limit that depends on how heavy the model is (a light model allows many more); at the limit the brush stops and says so;
  - while you paint the view adds only the new objects, instead of making them all again at every touch.
  - The radius and the distance stay as you chose them.
- The Simpler parts of the library (3D models ▸ Simpler and Vegetation ▸ Simpler) can become read only: red folders, no import, move, rename, conversion or deletion. It is switched on with «SimplerLocked»: 1 in the settings; with 0 (as now) they are normal folders.
- The bulb menu shows an icon for each kind of light (Omni, Spot, Area, Disc, LED strip, Sun); the other menu entries with an icon now show it too.
- Pivot moved by hand (violet gizmo): it stays where you put it also when you select the model again by clicking it in the view. On a library model it holds in the library too: new copies and the Scatter brush stand it on that point, and the distributions already made with that model adjust by themselves. Dragging the yellow square of the gizmo also rests the model on the pivot, not on its lowest point. Going back to the automatic pivot returns to the lowest point.
- Shorter top bar: Clay, Real time and Path tracing are in one button with a menu, showing the view in use. There is more room for the other buttons.
- Materials ▸ Simpler is now the «Simpler» folder of the material library, as for 3D models and vegetation: for now it is used as a normal folder and, with «SimplerLocked»: 1, it too becomes red and read only. Materials ▸ Mine shows everything else.
- After «Save to library» (and after an import) the library shows the folder where the model went, also when it is in Vegetation or in 3D models ▸ Simpler: before it always went back to 3D models ▸ Mine. «Save to library» proposes the folder you are looking at.
- Narrower top bar: when the window is not wide enough and the panel buttons keep only their icon, they shrink around the icon; more room is left for the others (the quality and the shadows read in full).
- Imported plants (for example from PlantFactory): the cut-out maps of leaves and flowers are read the right way round. The same name («Transparency») can mean a map that is white where there is no surface or one that is white where there is: Simpler now tells them apart from the border of the map (the border of a card is always empty). Before, some plants had solid cards with a hole in the shape of the leaf or spike. It applies to new imports and to «Convert to vegetation» on models already imported.
- Imported materials: changing any value (for example the season of the leaves) no longer turns the material bright white. The black emission of imported files is read as off, and the white light colour is set only when you switch «Emits light» on yourself. On materials that already turned white: switch «Emits light» off or use «Update from the library».
- Imports, thumbnails, conversions and renders run at full speed also while you use another program. Windows 11 moved Simpler's work to the slow cores as soon as its window was not in front. The priority stays below the program you are using, which keeps answering at once.
- Vegetation library: the «Import vegetation…» tile comes first, before the folders.
- Scatter: new «Delete» button next to «New». It removes the outline or line you are drawing with Area or Spacing (until it has models it is not in the scene list), or the scatter you are editing (Ctrl+Z brings it back). The chosen models stay.
- Real-time view: every plant shows its own pictures. Imported plants have pictures with generic names («color map.015») that come back for another picture when models are loaded and removed: the view took another plant's picture (flowers yellow in path tracing and violet in real time, white leaves, striped trunks). Cut-out masks in GIF now show in real time too (before, solid leaf cards) and become PNG next to the model at the import.
- Library: the folder path is on its own row, above the menus and from the left: readable also with many subfolders (when longer than the row, its end shows). The 3D view gives up a few pixels.
- AI video: new choice «Start and end» / «Start only». With «Start only» one keyframe and the description of what happens are enough (who walks, who moves away, who plays): the video starts from the picture and follows the text, without having to come back to a last picture. «Create/improve with AI» writes the text looking at that keyframe. Same models and same cost.
- Import into the library: choosing the folder is a clear tree (3D models and Vegetation with their branches, icons, branches that open and close), opened on the chosen folder and with «New folder here…». It starts from the folder the library shows at that moment. The Simpler folders do not appear when they are locked. Fix model materials: the models list is taller, the materials list shorter.
- Going from path tracing to real time shows «Busy · please wait – Switching to real time» at once until the view is ready: the materials are prepared in the background first and the notice stays until the first frame (before, the view stood still even for tens of seconds without a word).
- Painted snow and puddles, sculpted snow: each stroke no longer makes the shelter from the rain, the coast and the drops be computed again (no shape of the scene changes); only the snow mantle is made again.
- Snow brush: while it is on the mantle is computed lighter (detail at most 40%, the slider does not change) and only after a short pause between strokes: many strokes, one computation. With the brush off the mantle goes back to the detail chosen; final images always have it.
- The ring of the snow brush is bright blue and the one of «Sculpt the snow» violet: before, white and pale blue, they could not be seen on the snow.
- Real-time view: library models are shown with their Simpler materials as in path tracing (an inverted opacity map, Foliage, Fabric). Before, the cards of the spikes stayed solid in the view even when path tracing showed them right.
- Renaming a model in Vegetation ▸ Simpler or in 3D models ▸ Simpler now shows at once (the name changed, but the library still showed the old one).
- Decals ▸ Simpler: decals have the «Simpler» folder too (inside the folder of your decals), as models, vegetation and materials. For now it is a normal folder; with «SimplerLocked»: 1 it becomes red and read only.
- The mouse hourglass no longer stays on after the work is done while a library window is open (for example the import wizard).
- Real-time view: edits to the materials of a library model made of a single object (the Foliage season, the colour…) now show at once; before they stayed only in path tracing.
- Real-time view: no more fuchsia leaves on trees that give their pictures the same names (for example Maxtree): each material uses its own pictures even when Blender renumbers them.
- The scene thumbnail is updated with the path tracing also when Simpler recalculates the framed area before computing it.
- 3D models ▸ Simpler: the basic shapes (cube, sphere…) are in the «Primitives» folder.
- Mouse: when Simpler computes in the background and you can keep working (change panel, turn the view, read the settings) the arrow with the hourglass shows; the full hourglass stays only when the commands are really locked.
- Snow: along the kerb (and low steps, up to 30 cm) the mantle no longer has square cuts: the snow goes over the edge smoothly even when the sidewalk is not straight with the scene.
- Real-time view: the pieces of distributions (for example cars) no longer drop to the lowest detail when the view is fast enough. They get lighter only under about 20 frames per second; with the camera still they keep a minimum; the frames that prepare materials no longer count.
- Model packs: «Import ▸ Install a Simpler pack…» opens the pack (.simplerpack) in a window with its cover, title and description; after accepting the licence, the models go into the Simpler parts of the library, in the folders chosen by whoever made the pack. Simpler checks the signature and every file: a changed pack does not install. «Installed packs…» shows the list, with the licence and «Uninstall»; a new version updates the installed one.
- Library: deleting a folder that holds models, Simpler asks whether to delete the models too (with the subfolders) or only the folder, with the models going to the folder above. Before, the models always stayed and went to the folder above.
- Locked Simpler folders: Simpler's models and plants can only be inserted, scattered and copied into one's own folders. Rename, move, cut, delete, thumbnail, orientation, material fix and conversion leave their menus; nothing is moved or pasted into the Simpler folders. The library clean-up and the restore of material fixes leave them as they are too.
- Licence: Simpler is now free and open source software, under the GNU GPL version 3 or later (application and engine). The agreement, now version 2.0, explains in plain words what the GPL allows; the included models and the packs keep their own licence, and the name and logo cannot present modified versions as official. At the first start after the update the new agreement is asked once.
- At the first start the side panels are better balanced with the view: the left one a little narrower, the right one a little wider. Then they stay as you arrange them.
- Updates: Simpler checks by itself, at start and at most once a day, whether there is a new version. It does not slow the start and, if the website does not answer, it says nothing. When there is one, a discreet strip at the bottom offers «What's new», «Download», «Later» and «Skip this version»; if the version in use is no longer up to date the notice is more visible, but Simpler can be used all the same. Simpler does not download or install anything by itself: «Download» opens the page in the browser. In Settings, «Updates» section, the automatic check can be switched off and «Check now» always shows the outcome. The website receives only the version number and the language.
