SIMPLER 1.79.0  —  16 November 2026
===================================

First official release. Ecosystems to open, edit and copy; 21 new Simpler materials; «Interiors» material; content downloaded with a click; update notices; installer with Blender included; real-time view without freezes; clean fog and rain in path tracing.

- Ecosystems: a click on the thumbnail opens the ecosystem in the Ecosystems panel, with its layers (scatters), the models of each and how much they weigh. «Place on the selected object» grows it on the selected object, as the click did before; dragging it onto the view works as before.
- Ecosystems: «Edit on the plot» puts the ecosystem back on the plot exactly as it was saved, one scatter per layer. In the Ecosystems panel every scatter of the plot has its own button: a click opens it in the Scatter panel, where you add or change its models and their weight: the scatter updates at once, every piece picks its model again among the chosen ones and stays in its place. With the brush you add pieces, with Shift you erase them (the original ones too), and you can change models and settings. «Save changes» updates the same ecosystem (same name and folder, new thumbnail); «Save as new» makes another one and leaves the one you started from as it was. Both buttons are also in the Scatter panel and in the properties of the plot and of its scatters, under «You are editing the ecosystem …». Locked Simpler ecosystems can only be looked at.
- Ecosystems: «Copy» in the right-click menu; «Paste» on a folder or on the empty part of the library makes a copy «(copy)», also in the same folder. It works on Simpler ecosystems too, to make your own version in Mine.
- Ecosystems: the menu has the Simpler and Mine entries, like 3D models and Vegetation.
- Library: the first-level folders show an icon in the middle that tells what they hold (animals, people, furniture, garden, office, trees, flowers, metals, glass, lights, buildings…), both Simpler's red ones and your yellow ones, in 3D models, Vegetation, Materials, Decals and Ecosystems. The icon comes from the folder name, in Italian or English; subfolders stay plain.
- Scene: the right-click menu (in the tree and in the view) shows only the entries that apply to that object. On a distribution or an ecosystem there is no longer «Optimize polygons», «Convert to vegetation», «Find missing textures» or «New group inside», and «Save to library…» saves an ecosystem. On the zones of a terrain, the ground and the layers of an ecosystem there is no «Duplicate», «Remove from group» or «Delete». On lights and cameras only the entries that concern them remain.
- Ecosystems: an ecosystem placed in the scene can be changed in that project only, without touching the library. In its panel, «Layers of this ecosystem – only in this project» shows each layer with its models: presence, scale, rotation and season per model, remove a model (its pieces disappear), add one from the library (it takes a share of the pieces), switch a layer off, density per layer. «Back to the library version» removes the changes, «Save to the library as new» makes a new ecosystem of it. Ctrl+Z undoes every change.
- Simpler materials: 21 new materials and the Paints, Plastics & Rubber and Lights categories.
- Favourites: Vegetation has its own green folder, after 3D models.
- Library: «New folder» is always the first on the left, in every menu (3D models, Vegetation, Materials, Decals, Ecosystems).
- Decals: the thumbnails have a white background, so dark decals show well too.
- Decals: new «Max angle». Where the surface turns more than that angle away from the decal, the decal fades softly: a track on the road no longer smears onto a car tyre or a step inside its box. New decals start at 70°; at 90° the decal prints on everything, rounded objects too. Decals already placed stay at 90°, as before. It shows in the real-time view and in the render.
- Fixed: a library decal changed after it had been used in a project was placed again in that project with the old picture. Now it takes the new one.
- Fixed: the material eyedropper clicked on a floor with a decal on it (road lines, tyre tracks) said «No material on that surface». Now it looks past the decal and takes the floor's material.
- Fixed: a click on a panel slider without moving it sent a change (the value rounded by a hair) and Simpler updated the scene for nothing. Now nothing happens until the value changes.
- Fixed: a click on an object's gizmo without moving it made the view and the engine update the materials and the shelter from the rain («Updating the materials»). Now this happens only when the object really moves.
- Simpler decals: two new folders. Footprints: a muddy shoe print, trails of muddy shoes and boots, dusty prints for dark floors, wet bare footprints. Tyre tracks: a tyre track in mud, car tracks in mud and on a wet road, tractor tracks and skid marks.
- Fixed: opening a scene saved with an HDRI deleted the ground and made it again, losing its material and the decals lying on it (the road lines, for example). Now the ground stays as it is. The same happened when undoing a change of background.
- Fixed: an object dragged onto the view while the view was busy (for example just after changing scene) was lost without a message after 2 seconds. Now Simpler waits showing «Busy · wait» and says so if the view does not answer.
- Fixed: textures in formats other than JPEG and PNG (TIFF, TGA, EXR, HDR, BMP, WebP, DDS, JPEG 2000 and others) could fail to reach the real-time view when the material was a Simpler one (for example the leaves of plants converted to vegetation): the leaves looked white and solid. Now the view gets a PNG copy with its transparency.
- Fixed: a deleted model could stay visible in the real-time view although it was no longer in the scene tree.
- Fixed: models imported with a picture without transparency connected to the opacity looked full of holes in the real-time view; now they are solid as in path tracing.
- Fixed: painting a new scatter (also the new layer of an ecosystem), the first touches of the brush put models on top of the ones just painted: trees on top of other trees. Now the pieces rest only on the ground and on the objects of the scene.
- Fixed: GLB/glTF models with leaves cut out by the transparency of the texture (alpha mask) were imported with solid, square leaves. Now the cut-out stays.
- Ecosystems: a click on the thumbnail opens the ecosystem on the plot, framed, with its scatters on the right; the thumbnail of the open one is highlighted. If the one open before has unsaved changes, Simpler asks whether to save them. «New ecosystem» empties the plot (with the same question). After «Save as new» the new one stays open. Locked Simpler ecosystems open only to look at.
- Fixed: saving an ecosystem with a scatter of the plot selected, the other scatters of the plot were not saved.
- Ecosystems: «Save as ecosystem» at the top of the properties and of the Scatter panel when an object with scatters, a scatter, an ecosystem placed in the scene or the plot is selected. It asks for the name and puts it in the Ecosystems library, in the open folder.
- Fixed: «Save to library…» (right click in the tree or in the view) on an object with scatters, on a scatter or on an ecosystem placed in the scene offered the folders of the 3D models. Now it saves it as an ecosystem, with the folders of the ecosystems. Saving from the Ecosystems panel also asks for name and folder.
- Ecosystems: «Leave the plot» in the blue note of the view and in the panels. It asks whether to save the changes, removes the plot with its scatters, shows the scene again and frames it (Ctrl+Z undoes it).
- Scatter: for vegetation models, under each model's Advanced, «Season» and «Random season». Each plant of the scatter takes its own season, for example 40% ± 20% gives green, yellow and orange trees mixed together. It shows in the real-time view and in the render, and stays in saved ecosystems. With both at 0% the season of the material stays.
- Materials: new type «Interiors», for the glass of windows and shop windows. Behind every window a room appears with its depth, without modelling it. Flat facades are split into rooms of the chosen width and height, with the depth and light of the room adjustable. Rooms start from the corner of the object, so a facade is not cut in half. Room sizes are in real metres also on objects enlarged with the scale, and they work on a plane stood upright too. With «Fits the model» the rooms stay as you set them when you switch it on; if you then change the scale of the model, they stretch, squash, grow and shrink with it. When the Ambience is dark a room stays almost unlit, with a very faint glow; at night rooms can have their lights on: «Lights on», «Dusk to dawn» (they switch on by themselves following the time of the Ambience), «Intensity», «Warmth» (from warm yellow to cool blue) and «Lit rooms» (how many have the light on). It shows in the real-time view and in the render. In «Room» you can load a room picture laid out as a cross split 3×3, EXR too: back wall, ceiling, floor and side walls make the room, while the two layers of furniture and the window frame, with their transparency, stand in front. The preview and the library thumbnail of an «Interiors» material show its room through a window, not the sphere. Without a picture there is a test room. Materials ▸ Simpler ▸ Interiors has 16 ready rooms, already set for day and night: two open-plan offices, two executive offices, two meeting rooms, two living rooms, two kitchens, two bedrooms, two shops, a bar and a restaurant.
- Scatter: «Season» and «Random season» show for every model of the Vegetation library, also for those converted before Simpler marked the leaves.
- Rendering ▸ Colour and exposure: new «Exposure from the framing». Like a camera's light meter, it measures the light of what the camera frames and opens up the exposure in shaded scenes: a forest at noon, a portico, an interior no longer come out almost black at exposure 0. It never darkens, at most +6 stops, it protects the brightest parts and does nothing at night. It works in the path tracing view and in the final render; the real-time view uses the last measurement. Exposure still adds to it. It is on in new projects; in projects already saved it stays off, so they stay as they were.
- Rendering: new projects start with the «Base contrast» tone, which keeps deep shadows more readable. Projects already saved keep theirs.
- Fixed: opening a scene right after the path tracing of another one had finished could put the previous scene's thumbnail on the scene just opened. Now the path traced thumbnail only goes to the scene it belongs to.
- Fixed: a panel slider could go back to its previous value without sending the change, or change when another slider was moved. It happened when the panel refreshed by itself while the slider was held or a number was being typed (for example at the end of the snow mantle computation). Now the panel waits until the slider is released or the number confirmed.
- Sculpted snow: where footprints, tyre tracks or digging are sculpted, the grass, tufts and small plants of distributions and ecosystems are pressed into the snow and disappear inside the print, with their snow. Before, the snow on the tufts covered the footprints. Outside the print everything stays as it is; clearing the sculpted snow, undoing or removing the snow brings them all back. It works in the real-time view and in path tracing.
- Fixed: opening a project with ecosystems, trees and grass could appear pink in path tracing until the ecosystem was recalculated. Now on opening Simpler recalculates every ecosystem of the project with its own settings, before the first picture: opening projects with ecosystems takes a few seconds longer.
- «Why Simpler exists»: new «Roadmap» tile, before «Free models». It opens the roadmap of the Simpler website in the language of Simpler, in the space of the view. «Free models» now lists, by kind, the websites where to find free models and plants (among them Quixel Megascans on Fab, Sketchfab, Poly Haven, Maxtree, BlenderKit, Smithsonian 3D and NASA), with the licence, format and account of each.
- Packages: in the import menu a separate section with «Packages…» and «Install a package from a file…». «Packages…» shows in one window all the packages published on the website and those already installed, each with its state (Installed, New, Update) and the filters All, Installed and To install. For each package there are Details (cover, description, destination folders, thumbnails of the models), Install or Update, Licence and Uninstall. «Install» downloads the package from the website, checks its size and SHA-256 fingerprint, then shows the licence and verifies the signature as for a package taken from disk. Once a day, after the update check, a blue strip announces new packages or updates of installed ones, once for each. In Simpler Pack Studio, «Mark released» prepares by itself the folder to upload to the website (packs.json, package files, images); the first time you choose the folder with «Prepare for the site…».
- Installer: one file installs Simpler with Blender and everything it needs, with a guided setup in English and Italian. It shows the graphics card it found, asks where to keep the libraries and has you accept the licence agreement: Simpler no longer asks for it on first start. It uninstalls from Windows like any other program.
- Downloadable content: Simpler's models, vegetation and ecosystems download at the first click, like those from Poly Haven. The cloud shows what is still to download, the green tick what is already on the computer; «Update all» brings the new versions. The installer stays light.
- «Simpler» library folders (red): they are read-only, so the content that comes with Simpler cannot be spoiled by mistake.
- Your libraries start empty: Simpler creates no folders of its own in «Mine». Downloaded plants always go to the Vegetation section, in English as in Italian.
- Updates: Simpler tells you when a new version is out and opens the page to download it. You can also check by hand in File ▸ Settings ▸ Updates ▸ «Check now».
- Optional diagnostics (File ▸ Diagnostics…): if you choose so, Simpler sends the author the lines about problems, without names, folders, emails or images. You choose between «Automatic», «Only when I decide» and «Do not send»; it starts on «Only when I decide».
- Example projects: the two example scenes open from the start screen. Simpler makes a copy in the «Examples» folder next to your library; if you delete it, it is made again at the next click.
- Real-time view without freezes: opening a project, changing scene, turning on rain or fog, selecting an object or putting glass in the scene no longer stops the view for tens of seconds. The lights of the view have a fixed number and all materials (shadows and what is seen through glass included) are prepared before drawing. Path tracing is unchanged: it always uses all the lights.
- If the graphics card resets the 3D view, Simpler restarts it by itself and puts the framing back where it was.
- Path tracing: the label shows the pass (1/5…5/5), the samples done and the time, so you can see it is working.
- Fog in path tracing: no more black dots. With rain in the fog the drops were dark specks, and in thick fog the edges of trees and things seen behind clouds were left without fog. Now every point gets the fog of its own distance.
- Panel sliders: a moved value is always sent, even if the mouse is released outside the slider or the panel refreshes while you hold it. The right panel no longer stays stuck (for example on Scatter) when you choose another panel.
- Top bar: on small screens or with Windows at 125-150% it shrinks as much as needed, so the panel buttons always stay visible.
- Scene tree: «Group into a new group» (Ctrl+G) and «New empty group», also with the right button on the empty part of the tree. A car grouped with its lights is saved in the library as one model, lights included.
- Right-click menu redone: it is the same in the tree and in the view, works on the whole selection and shows only the commands that apply to all the selected objects, in sections always in the same order. «Merge into a single object» appears only when it can merge something.
- Paste where you click: on a group «Paste inside…», on an object next to it, on the empty part of the tree at the top. Ctrl+V stays as it was.
- Scatter brush: the limit is 300,000 pieces per distribution with any model (before, with heavy models such as trees, it stopped at 2,000). You can also paint on models placed from the library, for example a mountain, and Area uses them as a surface.
- Gizmo: looking closely at a flat object almost edge-on, the light-blue plane area became huge and covered the rotation arcs. Now it keeps the size of the gizmo and the arcs can always be grabbed.
- Final render ▸ Textures: new «1K» choice, the lightest for the graphics card in very large scenes.
- LUTs: while they are being prepared, the thumbnails show a grey «Preview coming…» tile instead of wrong colours.
- Start screen: the status text sits above the blue band and no longer covers the small print below.
- Roadmap: it opens a page with the roadmap only, without menus or links to the rest of the website.
- Packs: the download goes through the counter of the website (it counts only date, pack, version and language; no data about who downloads).
- Caustics: only two choices, «Off» and «On» (the former «Sharp»). «Normal» has been removed: with spots, point lights and the sun it gave the same picture as «Off» and was confusing.
