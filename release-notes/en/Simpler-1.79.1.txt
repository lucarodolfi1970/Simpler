SIMPLER 1.79.1  —  20 November 2026
===================================

Fixed the missing ground texture when opening the examples on another PC; projects remember which version of Simpler saved them.

- Fixed: opening on another PC, or as another Windows user, a project with Simpler's ground (the example scenes too), Simpler asked for a missing ground texture («piastrelle_v1.jpg»). The ground textures are Simpler's own: now it finds them by itself.
- Projects: every project saved remembers the version of Simpler that saved it. If you open it with an older version, it opens anyway and Simpler warns that some things may be missing or not look as expected, suggesting to update. Projects saved before have no version and open as always, without warnings.
