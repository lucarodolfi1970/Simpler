SIMPLER 1.79.2  —  26 November 2026
===================================

Video tutorials in the start box; fixed terrain zones not drawn in the real-time view.

- New: in the start box, between the examples and «Suggest an idea», there is «Video tutorials»: it opens the videos page of the Simpler website in the space of the view, in English or Italian according to the language of Simpler. The videos play there, full screen too.
- Fixed: in the real-time view some zones of a terrain could be left undrawn, when a zone blended towards many others with rain or snow on. Now the zone gives up one edge blend at a time until the graphics card accepts the material.
- Fixed: with the Ground raised to make the water of a river or a lake (a water or glass material from the library), one centimetre of snow «Thickness» laid a white sheet over the water and over everything lower than it. Now a see-through Ground, or one set to «Always dry» for the snow, takes no mantle.
- Fixed: with the Ground tilted (by one degree, say, for a river flowing downhill) the snow mantle stayed level at the height of its centre: on the lower side it stood metres above the Ground and covered rocks and river with just one centimetre of «Thickness». Now the mantle follows the plane of the Ground.
- Fixed: some Poly Haven materials that come in several colour variants (Fabric Pattern 05 and 07, for example) would not download («no colour map»). Now the first colour variant is used.
- Fixed: in the «Haze and fog» panel, while the engine was working after a change of density, the «Visibility about …» line still showed the previous value (for example density 0 and «260 m» together). Now it follows the chosen density at once.
- Fixed: with two copies of Simpler open together, or Simpler and Pack Studio open together, one could take away the other's files of the 3D view, rain and snow; at start it could stay on «Preparing the 3D view» for ever.
- New: from the menu of the light-bulb button a light can also be dragged into the view and dropped where you want, on the surface under the pointer. A click works as before: the light goes to the middle of the view.
- New: in the start box, at the end, the «GitHub» tile opens the page of Simpler on GitHub in the browser.
- Fixed: saving a scene right after opening a project with water reflecting light on the walls (pools), the thumbnail of the scene could come out all pink.
- New: when importing a model into the scene, the dialog shows its size before the import and lets you change its scale: a factor (with quick buttons, for example ×0.01, or ×2.54 for inches) or the height it must have. With several files together the same scale applies to all, so they stay aligned.
- Fixed: an animated model (for example from Sketchfab or Mixamo, with a skeleton) could have different poses in the real-time view and in path tracing: standing in one, lying in the other. Now the pose of the first frame is kept on import, the same everywhere; Simpler says so with a notice.
- Fixed: when the render engine stopped, Simpler always blamed memory. Now, if Blender crashes because of an internal error, the message says so and tells where the error report is.
- Fixed: changing the shadow quality of the real-time view (Off, Medium, High, Ultra) turned the view all grey, and it stayed so until «Off» was chosen. Now you can switch between levels.
- Fixed: on a narrow screen (low resolution, enlarged text, side panels open) the row of tiles of the start page did not fit and the last ones were cut off. Now the row scrolls sideways, with its bar under it and the mouse wheel.
