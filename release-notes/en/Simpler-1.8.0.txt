SIMPLER 1.8.0  —  21 October 2026
=================================

POINT-BASED SCATTERS: LAWNS AND WOODS WITH HUNDREDS OF THOUSANDS OF PIECES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- A scatter is no longer made of one object per piece, but of a single point object: each point says which
  model to show and how it is placed (position, rotation, scale). Blender draws them as instances with Geometry
  Nodes ("Instance on Points"): same result in path tracing and in the final render.
- Speed (measured without a graphics card):
  · redoing a scatter of 20,000 pieces: 0.2 s; 50,000: less than 1 s (previously 1-2 s for 3,000);
  · undo and redo: instant;
  · the scene list and the tree no longer grow with the pieces: the scatter is a single row and shows
    how many pieces it has.
- Real-time view: the points arrive in one block, in binary. The view draws them with the same instancing and
  levels of detail as before. In addition, pieces that are tiny on screen (a distant blade of grass) are not
  drawn, and the graphics card stays smooth even with a huge number of pieces.
- The brush finds surfaces with a search tree (three-mesh-bvh): brush strokes stay fast even
  over heavy models.
- Scatters in projects made with earlier versions are converted automatically when the project is opened,
  together with their brush strokes.

WHAT CHANGES IN USE
-------------------
- Clicking a piece selects its scatter (the gizmo moves all of it). Individual pieces can no longer be moved by
  hand: you change them with the brush, Shift to erase, and with the adjustments.
- Materials: drag a material onto a piece and it changes on all the pieces of that model, only on the surfaces
  that had the material under the mouse (with Shift, on all the surfaces of the model). Changes made in the
  material editor also show up immediately on scatters.
- Hiding a scatter from the tree hides all its pieces, in path tracing too.
