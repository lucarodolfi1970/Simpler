SIMPLER 1.8.1  —  21 October 2026
=================================

NEW
---
- MOVE ON SURFACES, SLOPED ONES TOO: when you drag the centre of the gizmo, the object not only rests on the
  surface but also tilts like the surface under the mouse (a book on a lectern, a rock on a slope). On
  horizontal surfaces it stays upright as before. Alt: free movement.

BUILD
-----
- At the end of the build, Build-App.bat starts Simpler and closes by itself: the window stays open only if the
  build fails, so you can copy the error.
- New "Compila e avvia.vbs" (double-click): builds without any window and starts Simpler. If something goes
  wrong, it shows a warning and opens build.log to copy to Claude.
