SIMPLER 1.8.2  —  21 October 2026
=================================

- MOVE ON SURFACES: an object tilted on a sloping surface and then put back on the floor (or on any level
  surface) goes back upright. Previously it stayed crooked: the tilt it had picked up remained. Now the object
  always follows the surface under the mouse, relative to its "up" direction, and rests on its true base.
- "ANT" SPEED: new button to the left of "walking", with steps 5 times smaller (wheel and arrow keys), for
  getting close to details.
