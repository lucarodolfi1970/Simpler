SIMPLER 1.8.4  —  21 October 2026
=================================

- LED STRIP: in path tracing it looked switched off because Cycles, by default, does not show the surface of an
  area light in the image: the strip lit its surroundings but the glowing line could not be seen. Now the LED
  strip is created "Visible to camera": in path tracing you see the lit line and the real-time view shows it the
  same way (only from the side it emits towards, as in Cycles).
- AREA LIGHTS: new "Visible to camera" option in the light panel (Area, Disc, LED strip). On: the glowing
  surface is visible; off: it lights the scene but does not appear in the image (suited to softbox panels).
  For strips already inserted, just turn it on.
- PROJECTS: when reopened, a project shows the same framing it had when saved, instead of framing the
  whole scene. Projects saved without a framing still frame everything.
