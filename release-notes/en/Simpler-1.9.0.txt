SIMPLER 1.9.0  —  21 October 2026
=================================

- SCATTER > AREA: the second mode of the Scatter tool. You draw the outline by clicking
  on surfaces (click on the first point, double-click, Enter or Esc to close it; Backspace removes the last point)
  and the area fills with the models chosen in the library, with their presences.
  · Afterwards: drag the points (they slide over the surfaces), the "+" halfway along each segment adds a point,
    Del removes the selected one. The area updates immediately.
  · Adjustments: Spacing, Random spacing, Probability (density), Tension (soft outline or straight
    segments), Edge falloff with its Falloff distance (towards the outline, objects become sparser and
    smaller), Snap to surfaces, Random seed with "Change" (same area, different random choices).
    Scale, rotation (random ones too) and Align to surface are shared by Brush and Area.
  · Objects drop onto the first surface below the area; walls and very steep faces stay empty.
  · Properties of an object > Scatter > "Exclude from Area": Area puts nothing on it and objects drop onto the
    surface below. "Update" recomputes the area after moving or excluding objects.
  · Performance as with the brush: in Cycles they are instances (points), in the view GPU instances with levels
    of detail; even hundreds of thousands of objects. The adjustments change the area afterwards too, and
    everything can be undone with Ctrl+Z.
  · A scatter made with Area, chosen in the tree, reopens in Area with its outline.
